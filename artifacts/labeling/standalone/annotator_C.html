Keyboard shortcuts and label definitions
  • Relation: Q supports · W opposes · E mixed_or_conditional · R neutral_or_no_position · T unclear
  • Mode: A explicit_stance · S analytical_exposition · D voiced_continuation · F refusal · G empty · H incomplete · J other
  • Confidence: 1 · 2 · 3   Navigation: ← → · Enter = next incomplete · N = notes · Esc = leave notes
  • supports: argues for, endorses, or continues the statement as its own claim
  • opposes: argues against or rejects the statement
  • mixed_or_conditional: substantial support and opposition, or support only under conditions
  • neutral_or_no_position: describes or analyses without taking a side
  • unclear: cannot be determined
  • Responses may end mid-sentence (length limit); judge what is present.
